AI & Automation

AI that works in production.

AI doesn't replace good engineering. It depends on it. We connect AI to the systems and data you already have, and we're just as ready to tell you when conventional automation is the better answer.

Talk to an EngineerRelevant work
The problem

Why this is harder than it looks.

AI doesn't fix broken processes. Most failed AI projects are integration and data problems in disguise: the model works in a demo, but it can't reach the right data, can't be trusted without review, and nobody owns it once it's live.

We treat AI as one component in a production system — with access controls, logging, human oversight and a clear definition of what "working" means.

Approach

How we work.

  1. 01Start with the processFind where the manual effort actually is. Often the right answer is conventional automation.
  2. 02Connect to real dataAI is only as useful as the business data and systems it can safely reach.
  3. 03Keep a human in the loopMulti-step automation with review points where the stakes justify them.
  4. 04Run it like softwareDeployed, logged, access-controlled and monitored like everything else we ship.
