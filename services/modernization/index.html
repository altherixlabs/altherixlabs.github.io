02 · Modernization

Most companies don't need another rewrite.

Existing systems hold years of business rules that nobody wrote down. We modernise them deliberately — upgrading, refactoring and migrating in steps — instead of throwing them away.

Talk to an Engineer →Relevant work
The problem

Why this is harder than it looks

A full rewrite looks clean on a slide and is usually the riskiest option on the table. It discards knowledge encoded in the old system, stalls new features for months and often recreates the same problems on a newer stack.

Modernisation should reduce risk, not move it. We start with what already works and change what doesn't.

How we approach it

Our approach

  1. Understand what's thereMap the existing system, its data and the business rules it quietly enforces.
  2. Change in stepsStack upgrades and architecture refactoring delivered incrementally, without stopping operations.
  3. Move when it paysCloud migration planned around cost, risk and what the workload actually needs.
  4. Make it fastProfile first, then fix: query tuning, caching and scaling where the evidence points.
