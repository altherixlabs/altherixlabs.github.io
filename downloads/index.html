Installing

A one-time check, then you're set.

Lumen is a new app from a small team, so macOS and Windows don't recognise it yet and ask you to confirm the first time you open it. These are the exact messages you'll see and what to do.

On a Mac

  1. 01InstallOpen the downloaded .dmg and drag Altherix Lumen into the Applications folder. Then open it from Applications.
  2. 02“Apple could not verify ‘Altherix Lumen’…”This message is expected the first time. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  3. 03Allow it onceOpen System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to the bottom. Next to “Altherix Lumen” was blocked, click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  4. 04That's itLumen now opens normally on this Mac. A new version may ask once more after you install it.

On Windows

  1. 01InstallOpen the downloaded Altherix-Lumen…setup.exe.
  2. 02“Windows protected your PC”Click More info, then Run anyway. Accept the licence agreement and finish the setup.
  3. 03“Smart App Control blocked an app…”Some new Windows 11 PCs have Smart App Control switched on, which blocks new apps without offering Run anyway. Email us and we'll help you get set up. Please don't switch Smart App Control off just for Lumen: Windows can't turn it back on without a reset.
  4. 04Your songs are safeUninstalling or reinstalling Lumen never deletes your song library.
Try it, then license it

Every feature, from the first minute. No time limit.

Without a licence, Lumen shows a small “Unlicensed” mark in the corner of the projected screen. When you're ready, we'll send your church a licence key. Paste it into Settings → License and the mark disappears. Activation works offline too.

Request a licence

Or email contact@altherix.in with the subject “Altherix Lumen licence”. Stuck installing? We'll help.

What's inside

Made for the person at the back of the room.

Songs in seconds

Paste lyrics from anywhere and each verse becomes a slide. Website clutter is removed for you. Search every title and lyric instantly.

Built for the live moment

A service list for today, a preview of what comes next, and Space, arrow keys or a presentation clicker to move on. A reused chorus is never duplicated.

Looks right on any screen

Themes with colour, gradient, image or looping video backgrounds. Text shrinks to fit and stays sharp from 1080p to 4K.

Calm when things go wrong

Black screen, clear text and logo at a keypress. If the projector cable is pulled, the output moves to a window instead of crashing.

Your songs stay yours

Everything lives on your computer: no account, no internet, no tracking. Back up the whole library to one file and restore it anywhere.

Bring your library

Import plain text files in bulk, and export to text or JSON whenever you like.

Questions before you install?

Ask about licences, setup for your church, or moving songs over from another program. An engineer will reply.
