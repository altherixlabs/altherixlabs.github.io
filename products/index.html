CodeGate by Altherix

Rank candidates on the code they write, not the CV they send.

CodeGate is an online coding assessment. Candidates solve real-world problems in a timed browser editor, and hidden tests plus AI grading turn every answer into a score you can rank.

Try CodeGate

Built for our own hiring rounds. JavaScript today, more languages planned.

Private, timed tests

Each candidate gets a single-use link by email. The timer runs on the server, work autosaves, and answers submit at time-up.

Tests decide correctness

Hidden test cases score whether the code works. The AI can't change that part of the score.

AI grades the rest

Edge cases, code quality and efficiency are scored against a written rubric, with a short summary of each answer.

Signals, not guesswork

Tab switches, pastes and copies are logged, and follow-up questions are generated about each candidate's own code.

How CodeGate scores

Ten points per question. The tests decide what works.

4Correctness, from hidden tests
2Edge cases
2Code quality
2Efficiency

Correctness comes only from hidden tests. AI scores the other six points against a written rubric.

  1. 01InviteAdd candidates. Each one gets a private test link by email.
  2. 02TestCandidates solve real-world problems in a browser code editor, with sample tests they can run.
  3. 03GradeOne click runs the hidden tests and AI-grades every answer.
  4. 04ShortlistRank candidates by score, review the flags, and use the generated questions on a short follow-up call.

HRMS by Altherix

Run your people operations from one place, not five spreadsheets.

HRMS is a lightweight HR system for small companies in India. It covers employee records, attendance, leave, timesheets, tasks, hiring and payroll, with each person seeing only what their role allows.

Ask about HRMS

Built for and run on our own team. Designed for small teams in India.

Attendance and leave

Clock in and out, with approvals through the reporting line. Leave accrues monthly from each person's real start date, under a policy you can edit, with your own holiday calendar.

Time and tasks

Create projects, assign tasks and log time against them. Interns see only the projects they're assigned to.

Payroll and payslips

Salary components for each person, TDS from editable income-tax slabs for both regimes, and Professional Tax by half-year. Employees can download a payslip once the month is marked paid.

Hiring

A public application form with resume upload and a screen for your recruiters. Schedule CodeGate coding tests and see the scores come back on the application.

How it works

From joining to payslip. One system, one login.

PAN, bank details and salaries are encrypted, and every view of them is logged. Built with India’s DPDP Act in mind: consent notices, and retention rules for exited staff and closed applications.

  1. 01Add your teamAdd people, managers and start dates. Staff sign in with their Google Workspace account.
  2. 02Run the day to dayPeople clock in, apply for leave and log time, and managers approve.
  3. 03Run payrollReview the calculated TDS and Professional Tax, and adjust them if you need to.
  4. 04Publish payslipsMark the month paid. Payslips become available to each employee.

CMS by Altherix

Know your church: every member, visitor, service and offering in one place.

Altherix CMS is church management software for one church or many. It keeps members and families, visitors, events, attendance and giving together, makes sure every visitor gets a call, and gives pastors a clear picture of how the church is doing.

Ask about CMS

Runs in the browser on any computer or phone. Churches side by side, or a main church with campuses below it.

Members and families

Every member with their household, groups, baptism and marriage dates. Record a birth or a death in one step, and see each person's story on a timeline, from their first visit onwards.

Every visitor followed up

Pastors assign each new visitor to someone who calls them and logs how it went. Returning visitors are recognised by phone number, and Make a member keeps their history.

Attendance on a phone

Ushers tick names from their phone during the service and add visitors as they arrive. Weekly services repeat by themselves, so there's nothing to set up each week.

Giving you can trust

Tithes, offerings and other contributions, each in the church's own currency. Mistakes are voided with a reason, never deleted, and the finance dashboard compares this year with last.

How it works

One church or many. Everyone sees only their part.

Pastoral notes are for pastors only. Ushers see names, not contact details. Removing someone's role locks them out at once, profile pictures stay in private storage, and the audit log records which fields changed without storing the values.

  1. 01Set up your churchesAdd one church, or a main church with campuses and branches below it. Each sees its own data; a role at the top sees everything below.
  2. 02Give people rolesPastor, IT Admin, Finance Admin, Event Admin, Usher or Church Representative. Everyone signs in with Google and sees only what their role allows.
  3. 03Run SundayUshers mark attendance and add visitors. Finance records the offerings. The pastor's dashboard updates as it happens.
  4. 04Care for peopleThe pastoral care list shows who hasn't attended for a few weeks, which visitors still need a call, and which follow-ups are due.

Lumen by Altherix

The words on the screen, exactly when the congregation needs them.

Lumen is lyrics and Bible projection software for churches. Build today's service from your songs and Bible readings, then move through it slide by slide on a projector or TV. It runs on macOS and Windows and projects fully offline, with no account. Licensed copies back up their songs online, so a new computer gets them back.

Download LumenAsk about licences

Free to try with every feature. A licence removes the large “Unlicensed” watermark from the projector. On Windows, Lumen is also on the Microsoft Store.

Bibles in your language

Import any Bible from wordproject.org: Tamil, Hindi, English and many more. Type a reference such as John 3:16, or the book name in your own language, and show it verse by verse with the reference on screen.

Songs in seconds

Paste lyrics from anywhere and each verse becomes a slide. Website clutter is removed for you. Search every title and lyric instantly.

Built for the live moment

A service list for today, a preview of what comes next, and Space, arrow keys or a presentation clicker to move on. A reused chorus is never duplicated.

Looks right on any screen

Themes with colour, gradient, image or looping video backgrounds. Text shrinks to fit and stays sharp from 1080p to 4K.

How a Sunday works

Plan it on Saturday. Press Space on Sunday.

Calm when things go wrong

Black screen, clear text and logo at a keypress. If the projector cable is pulled, the output moves to a window instead of crashing.

Backed up with your licence

With a licence, every song is backed up online as you add it. Activate the same licence on a new computer and your songs come back. No account, no tracking, and you can switch it off. You can also back up the whole library to one file.

Bring your library

Import plain text files in bulk, and export to text or JSON whenever you like.

Two languages on one screen

Dual Language songs show every slide in two languages, such as Hindi with Hinglish or Tamil with English. Paste both or combine two songs you already have, and switch to either language with one click during the service.

Lyrics on your live stream

Streaming to YouTube, Facebook or Instagram with OBS? Lumen puts the words on the stream as a lower third over your cameras, or as the full projector picture, and can switch OBS scenes for you when a song or Bible passage comes up.

Hindi and Indian languages

Hindi lyrics display in a bundled Devanagari font on every computer, search works on whole Hindi and Tamil words, and Hindi section tags like [पद 1] and [कोरस] are understood.

Bibles, themes and media stay on your computer. With a licence, Lumen backs up your songs to Altherix so you can restore them on another computer, and you can switch this off in Settings. It collects no analytics or tracking data. You are responsible for your own song copyright licence, such as CCLI; Lumen shows the CCLI number in the footer.

  1. 01Add your songsType them, paste them from a website, or import a folder of text files. Tag verses and choruses if you like, and set the order they're sung in.
  2. 02Build today's serviceAdd songs to the service list and drag them into order. It's saved, so a restart mid-service loses nothing.
  3. 03Choose the lookPick a theme for the church, or a different one per song, with your own background image or looping video.
  4. 04Run it liveSpace, the arrow keys or a presentation clicker move through each song in sung order, then on to the next song.
  5. 05Read the BibleType a reference, or search for a word. Add passages to the service list next to the songs, then show them verse by verse with the same keys.
  6. 06Handle the momentB blanks the screen, C clears the words but keeps the background, L shows your logo, and Esc clears everything.

Mandate by Altherix

Your ERP is about to get an AI colleague. Who decides what it can do?

Mandate is a safe way to connect AI agents to the systems your business already runs on.

Request early access

Or email contact@altherix.in with the subject “Mandate”.

Works with what you run

ERP, SQL databases and in-house applications, made usable by AI agents.

Your permissions, not new ones

Agents see only what their user is allowed to see.

A person approves risky actions

Writing data, sending documents or moving money waits for approval.

Every action on record

Each request and change is logged and can be replayed.

How Mandate works

AI that works inside your systems, only within its mandate.

  1. 01An AI agent asksAn assistant wants to read a record or take an action in one of your systems.
  2. 02Mandate checksIs this user allowed? Does this action need approval? The request is logged either way.
  3. 03Your system respondsOnly permitted, approved actions reach the system. Everything else stops at Mandate.
