Why Altherix

What working here actually looks like.

01

You own the whole problem

Engineers here talk to clients, shape the solution, build it and see it through to deployment. No handing a spec down a chain and hoping.

02

Real systems, real users

The platforms we build run production workloads for organisations that depend on them. Your code is used, not shelved.

03

Breadth by design

Consulting means variety — .NET one quarter, Laravel the next, an Azure migration after that.

04

Senior people to learn from

A deliberately small team with deep enterprise and architecture experience. Reviews are thorough and mentoring is direct.

How we hire

Four steps. No trick questions.

The process usually takes two to three weeks, and you'll hear from us at every stage.

STEP 01

Application

Send your CV and a few lines about what you've built. We read every one and reply within a week.

STEP 02

Intro call

Thirty minutes with an engineer. Your background, our work, and whether the fit makes sense both ways.

STEP 03

Technical conversation

We walk through a realistic problem together and discuss code you've written. No whiteboard algorithms, no unpaid take-home projects.

STEP 04

Offer

A final conversation on scope, expectations and compensation, then a written offer.

The package

What we offer beyond the pay.

Paid intern onsite

Interns join the team for a paid three-day onsite in Ooty — time to meet your mentor and the people you've been shipping with.

Hybrid working

Full-time roles split between our Nilgiris office and home, arranged around delivery rather than a fixed rota.

Learning budget

An annual allowance for certifications, courses and conferences, plus time to use it.

Health cover

Medical insurance for you and your immediate family.

Client-facing exposure

You sit in the room where decisions are made — experience that's hard to get in a large delivery centre.

Sane delivery

We estimate honestly and push back on unrealistic dates. Crunch is a planning failure, not a culture.
