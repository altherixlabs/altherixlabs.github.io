The Church of Pentecost —
two platforms, one connected estate

A multi-year engagement building the digital backbone for a global denomination: a congregation management platform for day-to-day assembly operations, and a missions reporting system that consolidates statistical returns across every tier of the organisation.

ClientThe Church of Pentecost
SectorNon-profit · Faith organisation
EngagementDesign, build, deploy & support
Platforms2 production systems
Platform 01

Church Management System

Membership · Events · Attendance · Analytics

A full congregation management platform replacing spreadsheets and paper registers with a single record of every member, group, event and attendance across the organisation's assemblies.

  • Member lifecycle — records, education and career history, salvation and baptism status, household and home-cell membership
  • Inter-assembly transfers — request workflow with automatically generated transfer letters
  • Events & recurrence — recurring services expanded into occurrences by a scheduled background job, with per-occurrence exceptions
  • Live check-in — real-time attendance capture over WebSockets, plus visitor and anonymous-attendee handling
  • Bulk data — Excel import and export for migrating existing registers
  • Analytics — dashboards and generated PDF reports for leadership
.NET 9 EF Core SQL Server SignalR Hangfire React 18 · TypeScript MUI Redux Toolkit Azure
Platform 02

Missions Office Enterprise System

Statutory reporting · Statistics · Governance

A reporting and analytics system for the global Missions Office, consolidating half-year and end-year statistical returns submitted from every level of a seven-tier organisational hierarchy.

  • Seven-level hierarchy — Global Office → Regional Missions Office → Regional Committee → Nation → Area → District → Local Assembly
  • Statistics dashboards — membership, evangelism, attendance, ministry and estates, each with drill-down from global down to a single assembly
  • Scoped access — enforced at query level, so an administrator only ever sees the branch of the hierarchy they are responsible for
  • Vision 2028 scorecard — configurable indicators and targets with pace-adjusted progress tracking
  • Exports & provenance — CSV and PDF exports stamped with scope, period and generation trail
  • Data completeness — tracks which assemblies have submitted for a reporting period and lists those outstanding
Laravel 12 PHP 8.2 MySQL Alpine.js Chart.js dompdf Azure App Service Azure DevOps
Live in production Deployed on Azure (UK South) through a gated release pipeline, with audit logging and role-based access throughout.
