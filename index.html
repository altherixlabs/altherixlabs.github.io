AI & Automation

AI that works in production.

AI doesn't replace good engineering. It depends on it.

Use AI
when the input is unstructured, the judgement is fuzzy, and a person can review what matters.
Use software
when the rules are known. Invoicing, reconciliation and reminders need solid automation, not a model.
Either way
it runs with access control, logging and an owner.

How we approach AI →

AI grading, checked by tests

In CodeGate, hidden tests decide whether code works. AI only scores quality against a written rubric and summarises each answer for a reviewer.

Agents behind a permission gate

Mandate, in development: an AI agent can act on ERP data only through approved actions, and risky ones wait for a person.

Automation that needs no model

In our client platforms, recurring events and data-completeness checks run as scheduled background jobs. No AI required.

A person can always see why

Where AI makes a judgement, the reasoning is shown and a person can override it.

Engineering practices

The unglamorous parts, done properly.

What goes into the production systems we run, as standard.

Security

  • Role-based access across production platforms
  • Hierarchy-scoped queries: users retrieve only their own data
  • Secrets kept out of code

Reliability

  • Logging, monitoring and alerting
  • Backups kept in good order
  • Scheduled work handled by background jobs

Delivery

  • CI/CD on Azure DevOps
  • Gated releases to production
  • Code review, with tests written alongside code

Governance

  • Audit trails on enterprise platforms
  • Exports stamped with scope, period and origin
  • Access enforced at the data layer, not only the UI
About

Global engineering. Rooted in the Nilgiris.

Altherix was founded in Ooty, high in the Nilgiris, and works from Coonoor in Tamil Nadu, India. It's an unusual place for an engineering company, and a deliberate one: a small team with room to do careful work, away from the churn of a large delivery centre.

The work reaches well beyond the hills. Platforms we built run in production on Azure for The Church of Pentecost in the UK and its Missions Office in Ghana. We take on projects across India, the UK and Europe.

One conviction hasn't changed: your existing software is the backbone of your business, and it usually deserves improving before it deserves replacing.

FAQ

Common questions.

01

What does Altherix do?

Altherix is a small software engineering company. We build custom web applications and enterprise platforms, modernize existing systems, add automation and AI where it helps, and connect business systems to each other. We also deploy and run what we build.

02

Why choose a focused team over a large agency?

You work directly with the engineers who design, build and run your system, with no account managers or hand-offs. Our platforms run in production on Azure for clients in the UK and Ghana, including a church management system with 80+ API modules and statistical reporting across seven organisational levels, and we'll walk you through how they're built.

03

Which technologies do you work with?

Our production work is in .NET 9 and ASP.NET Core, React and TypeScript, Laravel and PHP, SQL Server and MySQL, on Azure with Azure DevOps pipelines. We choose the stack that suits your system rather than forcing one.

04

How does an engagement start?

With a free 30-minute call with an engineer. We then review your existing system and send a written assessment with options, followed by a proposal: fixed scope and price for defined work, or a monthly team for ongoing work. Build work happens in two-week increments you can see and use.

05

How much does it cost, and how quickly can you deliver?

Our rates are well below those of UK, European and US agencies, and defined work gets a fixed price agreed before we start. You'll have working software within two weeks of starting, followed by a new increment every two weeks.

06

Can we start with something small?

Yes, and we recommend it. Start with a written assessment or a single module. If it works for you, we carry on; if not, you haven't committed to a large project.

07

Do you offer a referral discount?

Yes. If you refer a client to us, both you and the client you refer get a 5 to 10% discount.

08

Where is Altherix based?

Our office is in Coonoor, in the Nilgiris district of Tamil Nadu, India. Altherix was founded in nearby Ooty. We take on projects remotely across India, the UK and Europe.
