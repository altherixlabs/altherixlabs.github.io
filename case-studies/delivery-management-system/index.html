Delivery Management System

Real-time tracking, a delivery agent app, route optimisation and customer SMS, integrated with Google Maps.

CategoryLogistics · Mobile & platform
IndustryLogistics

Executive summary

A delivery management system with real-time tracking, a delivery agent mobile app, route optimisation and customer SMS notifications, integrated with Google Maps.

The challenge

A delivery operation needed real-time visibility of its agents, better routes and automatic updates to customers.

The approach

  • Put the tool in the agent's hand. A React Native mobile app for delivery agents.
  • See it live. Real-time tracking integrated with Google Maps.
  • Route better. Route optimisation for daily delivery runs.
  • Tell the customer. SMS notifications at the right moments.
