ERP Inventory Management Module

Inventory management inside the ERP — barcode scanning, real-time stock updates and automated reorder alerts.

Summary

A custom inventory management module for a mid-sized manufacturer's ERP, adding barcode scanning, real-time stock updates and automated reorder alerts.

The challenge

A mid-sized manufacturer needed its inventory handled inside the ERP, with stock levels kept current as goods move and reorders raised without relying on manual checks.

The approach

  • Capture at the source. Barcode scanning records stock movements as they happen.
  • Keep stock current. Stock levels update in real time as movements are recorded.
  • Automate the reorder. Reorder alerts are raised automatically when stock needs replenishing.
