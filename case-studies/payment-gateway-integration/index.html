Multi-Gateway Payment Integration

Razorpay, PayU and Paytm behind one integration, with UPI, wallets, EMI, an admin dashboard and automated reconciliation.

Category

Industry

Summary

A multi-gateway payment integration covering Razorpay, PayU and Paytm, supporting UPI, wallets and EMI, with an admin dashboard and automated reconciliation reports.

The challenge

An e-commerce business needed to offer customers more ways to pay and reconcile payments across several providers without doing it by hand.

The approach

  • Integrate once, route many. Razorpay, PayU and Paytm integrated behind a single payment layer.
  • Offer the methods customers use. UPI, wallets and EMI supported at checkout.
  • Reconcile automatically. Reconciliation reports generated automatically, with an admin dashboard for operations.
